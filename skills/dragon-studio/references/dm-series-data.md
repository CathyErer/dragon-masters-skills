## DM1 — 11-card story arc (drawing-first task cards)

World-building cards (#1–#5) = draw-frame + label grid + checks:
- #1 Meet Your Dragon Master (self-portrait) · #2 Design Your Dragon · #3 Name Your Dragon ·
  #4 Your Dragon's Power · #5 A Strange Dream (5 senses + cause→effect mini-sketches)

Story cards (#6–#11) = 4-panel comic board + one caption each (the 6-chapter story):
- #6 My Secret Plan (Ch1, show-don't-tell) · #7 A Quiet Moment (Ch2) · #8 Things Go Wrong (Ch3, because→so→finally) ·
  #9 Trapped! (Ch4, trap→try1❌→try2❌→cliffhanger) · #10 Hidden Power Revealed (Ch5 climax, small→BIG→saved→faces) ·
  #11 A New Beginning (Ch6) + Story Mountain SVG + Before/After draw-pair

Bridge: 📐 Story Planner (SWBST whole-story outline) — the skeleton Cards #6–#11 expand.
Final: bind all into 《My Dragon Master Book 1》 (cover + 11 cards + "To be continued in Book 2…").

## DM2 "Saving the Sun Dragon" — facts

- 14 chapters. DM2 = 续集冒险; students reuse the character / dragon / hidden power created in DM1.
- When starting DM2, design a 10-day plan + per-chapter skill map following the same
  shape as DM1's table above (14 chapters → 10 days, some days pair two chapters),
  or use a plan document the user provides.
- DM2 reading focus shifts from "how to read" (DM1 strategies) to "what to read" (story-element depth:
  character, theme, integrating clues, problem-solving, sequel prediction).
