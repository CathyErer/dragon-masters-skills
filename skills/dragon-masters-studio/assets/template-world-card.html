SKILL A · STORY ELEMENTS 故事三要素
A1What are Story Elements?什么是故事三要素？

Every story is built from three pieces. Good readers name all three while they read — if you can fill the three boxes, you understood the chapter.每个故事都由三块「积木」搭成。会读书的人边读边找这三块——三栏都能填出来，就说明读懂了这一章。

✕
🧑CHARACTER
The people or animals in the story.
故事里的人物或动物。
Ch 1: Drake, the soldier, Mom
✕
🏰SETTING
When and where the story takes place.
故事发生的时间和地点。
Ch 1: long ago · the Kingdom of Bracken — an onion field, then a castle
✕
⚡EVENTS
What happens in the story — beginning, middle, and end.
故事里发生的事——开头、中间、结尾。
Ch 1: a soldier takes Drake to the castle

SKILL B · CHARACTER TRAITS → OUTSIDE TRAITS 人物特征 → 外在特征
B1What are Character Traits? What are Outside Traits?什么是人物特征？什么是外在特征？
✕
🏷️CHARACTER TRAITS
Words that describe a character. There are two kinds: outside traits (what you can SEE) and inside traits (what's in the heart).
描述人物的词。分两种：outside traits（看得到的）和 inside traits（藏在心里的）。Inside traits 明天 Ch 2 学。
Example: tall = outside · brave = inside
✕
👀OUTSIDE TRAITS — today's focus
What a character looks like on the outside — things you can SEE: hair, clothes, size, what they carry or ride.
人物的外在特征——你能「看到」的：头发、衣服、高矮、带的东西、骑的马。
Ch 1: the soldier has a yellow beard — that's an outside trait!
