# Dragon Masters — series data (read before generating a chapter lesson or card)

Lesson plans are generated **one per chapter**: DM1 = 16 lessons, DM2 = 14 lessons.
Each chapter gets a main reading skill (SKILL A), an optional second skill
(SKILL B), and one drawing-first task card.

> **DM3 and later titles are NOT in this file.** Build their table with
> `design-standard.md` (same folder) — read the whole book, derive each chapter's
> skill from its own text, then write it up in the format below. Never invent a
> per-chapter table without the book.

## Provenance & the alternative 10-day pacing

These per-chapter tables are derived from a full two-book classroom run, where
the same material was taught on a **10-day schedule** with chapters paired:

- DM1: Day 1–3 = Ch 1/2/3 · Day 4 = Ch 4-5 · Day 5 = Ch 6-7 · Day 6 = Ch 8-9 ·
  Day 7 = Ch 10-11 · Day 8 = Ch 12-13 · Day 9 = Ch 14-15 · Day 10 = Ch 16
- DM2: Day 2 = Ch 2-3 · Day 7 = Ch 8-9 · Day 8 = Ch 10-11 · Day 9 = Ch 12-13;
  the rest one chapter per day

Two things follow from the per-chapter split:

1. **Restored skills.** Pairing chapters forced one skill per lesson, so three
   DM1 skills were dropped at the time — Ch2 Asking Questions, Ch6 Compare
   Backgrounds, Ch12 Predicting. With a full lesson per chapter they return as
   SKILL B (marked above).
2. **Split cards.** Cards that covered two chapters were split back to their own
   chapters — DM2 #2 → Ch2 + Ch3, #7 → Ch8 + Ch9, #8 → Ch10 + Ch11,
   #9 → Ch12 + Ch13.

If the user teaches on a fixed schedule, per-chapter lesson files pair back
naturally — teach two files on a two-chapter day.
